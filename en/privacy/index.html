Privacy

Last updated 03.10.2026 · Polski

Who is responsible

The data controller is Jacek Mariusz Taczała (a private individual, the author of the game), contact: personvern [at] stop60.no. The game has no accounts, no ads and no profiling, and no data is ever sold.

Cookie-free statistics

Visits are counted with GoatCounter (script hosted with the game): page and referring page, browser and system, language, screen width, country (derived from the IP address) and the events “game start” and “game end”. The IP address and browser are only held in server memory (up to 8 hours) to count unique visits; only aggregate statistics are stored, with no IP address or ID. GoatCounter stores nothing in your browser, and the game doesn't set cookies. Purpose: knowing how many people play and where they come from. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object, e.g. by blocking scripts or by emailing personvern [at] stop60.no.

Data on your device

The game stores the following in your browser's storage (localStorage): your nickname, the local leaderboard (nickname, points, number of bugs squashed), your best score, the sound setting, the "Skip the intro" setting, the language setting, your choice about the online leaderboard, and any scores waiting to be sent to the online leaderboard while you were offline. These aren't cookies and they don't leave your device (except for the online leaderboard, below). They're needed for features you use, so no consent is required.

Online leaderboard

You need a nickname to play. If you tick "Show my score on the online leaderboard" under the nickname field, the following goes on the public online leaderboard when a round ends: nickname, score (points, number of bugs squashed, wave), the game and the time it was saved; anyone can see the top 10. If you don't tick it, your score stays on your device only. Don't use your real name, email or anyone else's details – any made-up nickname will do. Nicknames are filtered automatically, and entries that break the rules are removed.

Purpose: running the leaderboard. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – the leaderboard is part of the game, taking part is optional and you can switch it off at any time.

Who receives data

Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore, part of the Supabase, Inc. group, USA) – the leaderboard database, on servers in the EU (AWS, Ireland), acting as a processor under a data processing agreement (DPA) that includes the EU standard contractual clauses in case of access from outside the EEA (e.g. technical support); its server logs may briefly record your IP address (Supabase privacy policy). GoatCounter (Martin Tournoij, Ireland) – statistics, Hetzner servers in Finland and Germany (GoatCounter privacy policy). E-mails you send to stop60.no addresses are forwarded by Domeneshop AS (Norway) to an inbox at Proton AG (Switzerland); we use them only to reply and delete them once the matter is closed.

GitHub, Inc. (USA) – GitHub Pages hosting; when pages are downloaded, GitHub may log your IP address and browser details (GitHub privacy statement). Fonts are hosted with the game (no Google Fonts).

How long

Online leaderboard entries: until deleted at your request, and for no more than 12 months after they were saved. GoatCounter statistics: aggregate data only, no personal data. Supabase and GitHub server logs: briefly, in line with their policies. Data on your device: until you delete it (clear this site's data in your browser settings).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Send a request (e.g. to delete a leaderboard entry) via personvern [at] stop60.no – give your nickname, the game (SNEAKY BUGS) and a rough date, and we'll delete the entry. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority, e.g. UODO (Poland) or Datatilsynet (Norway).

Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13. If you're under 16, use a nickname that doesn't give anything away about you, or don't switch on the online leaderboard.
